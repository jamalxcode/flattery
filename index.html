How the ratings work

Every eight hours Flattery searches X, Hacker News and Reddit for posts announcing a clone, a reverse-engineered or a reimagined version of existing software, follows builders whose projects did well, and looks up the posts that link each known repo. Claude Haiku keeps only real announcements, then reads the replies and quote posts for their tone and for red flags such as scams, stolen code or projects that don't work. GitHub stars and downloads count only for the project's own repo (posted by its maker or shared by others), never for a famous unrelated one a post happens to link.

🏛️ Established is a separate section: mature open-source apps that replace a paid one (Kdenlive for Premiere Pro, OBS Studio, GIMP, LibreOffice…), rated on their GitHub use: 1,000+ stars and a change in the last six months to be listed. Suggest one with #FlatteryAdd and its GitHub link.

Steady ratings: a new rating shows once it holds for two scans in a row, so cards don't flip back and forth. ☠️ Don't touch and big jumps show at once. Get buttons appear only for ❤️ Loved and ⭐ Proven apps with an established repo; the files come straight from the project's GitHub releases and aren't checked by Flattery.

  • ❤️ Loved: real use on GitHub and a real crowd on X, mostly positive.
  • ⭐ Proven on GitHub: lots of stars or downloads, but quiet on X so far.
  • 🔥 Hyped, unproven: popular on X with no repo, or one nobody uses yet.
  • 😐 Mixed: people are split.
  • 🆕 New & untested: too new or too small to judge.
  • 🌫️ Didn't catch on: two weeks on, with little reach and no real GitHub use. It can still come back.
  • ☠️ Don't touch: mostly negative replies, a pile-on, a removed repo, or a serious red flag raised by at least two different accounts, quoted on the card.

Missing a clone? Use ＋ Suggest a clone: paste its link (the X post announcing it, or its GitHub page) and submit. Or post the link on X with #FlatteryAdd. The next scan checks it and, for the form, replies with the result.

Everything here is crowd opinion, collected and summarised automatically. It is not a legal or technical judgement, and nobody reviews it by hand. If a listing is wrong, use ⚑ Object on X on its card: once six or more established accounts object for real reasons, spread over at least two hours, it is taken down automatically.
