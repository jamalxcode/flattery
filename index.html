How the ratings work

Every eight hours Flattery searches X for posts announcing a clone, a reverse-engineered or a reimagined version of existing software. Claude Haiku keeps only real announcements, then reads the replies and quote posts to judge the tone and spot red flags such as scams, stolen code or projects that don't work.

❤️ Loved means people actually use it: GitHub counts most. A repo with 1,000+ stars or 10,000+ downloads is loved however small its X post was; one with 100+ stars or 500+ downloads needs a decent reception on X too. Stars only count when the repo is the poster's own, or new enough to be the clone itself; linking someone else's famous project borrows nothing. 🔥 Hyped, unproven is popular on X but has no repo, or one nobody uses yet. 🆕 New & untested is too new or too small to judge. ☠️ Don't touch means mostly negative replies, a pile-on, a removed repo, or a serious red flag raised by several people.

This is crowd opinion, not a legal or technical judgement. Always check a project yourself before you run it.
