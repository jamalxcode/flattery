How to read this. Flattery searches X for posts announcing clones, reverse-engineered or reimagined versions of existing software, and Claude Haiku keeps only real announcements. The reception score combines likes, reposts and bookmarks, engagement per view, the tone of the replies and quote posts (read by Haiku), and the linked GitHub repo's stars, downloads and star growth. A post is well received only when GitHub confirms it (at least 100 stars or 500 release downloads) and people are mostly positive. Buzz, not confirmed means a popular post with no repo or one nobody uses yet. Stay away means mostly negative replies, a pile-on (many more replies than likes), a removed repo, or a serious red flag such as scam, malware or stolen code. Posts under 24 hours old or with little reach are too early to tell. This is crowd opinion, not a legal or technical judgement.
