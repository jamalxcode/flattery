What happens next

  • The next scan (every 8 hours) checks the link.
  • A clone is listed once people have reacted to it on X: Flattery rates clones by those reactions, with GitHub stars and downloads as proof.
  • A mature open-source app (1,000+ GitHub stars, updated in the last 6 months) that replaces a paid one goes into 🏛️ Established.
  • Anything else, like news, a list of tools or a library, isn't listed.
